04 · Motif

The flock.

A murmuration: thousands of particles holding the shape of a voice. On the site they fly into each artifact; in stills they hold the waveform. About 6% of particles are signal. Use it once per asset, never behind text.

07 · Rules

Never, on any asset.

  1. 01No generated or recreated product UI. Screens are always real captures from app.trymurmur.studio.
  2. 02No invented numbers, testimonials or customer logos. Every number traces to a real run.
  3. 03No sped-up footage without the honesty label and that footage's real run time.
  4. 04No SprintZero branding on Murmur assets.
  5. 05Signal at most once per asset. No gold: the v1 palette is retired.
